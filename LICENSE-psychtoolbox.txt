The tables of the CIE 2006 observer model in src/data.ts (cie2006Tables) are prepared from data files of
Psychtoolbox-3 (PsychColorimetricData/PsychColorimetricMatFiles: den_mac_bone.mat, T_log10coneabsorbance_ss.mat,
den_lens_cie_1.mat, den_lens_cie_2.mat), which Psychtoolbox-3 distributes under the following notice. The data
themselves are those of CIE 170-1:2006 (Tables 6.4 and 6.10) and of Stockman and Sharpe, from the CVRL database; see
DATA.md.
